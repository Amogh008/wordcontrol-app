DLT account support

Delete your account and data

You can permanently delete your DLT (Deutsche Learn Tool) account from either the mobile app or the DLT web app.

How to request deletion

  1. Open DLT and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Select Delete account and confirm the request.
Open DLT

Data that is deleted

Deletion permanently removes your DLT account, authentication information, saved vocabulary, and notes. The deletion cannot be undone.

This page provides a web-accessible deletion path for users who no longer have the mobile app installed. For more information about how DLT handles data, read the Privacy Policy.
