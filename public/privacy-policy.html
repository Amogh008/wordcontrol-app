DLT · Deutsche Learn Tool

Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 30, 2026

DLT (Deutsche Learn Tool) is a German-language learning app. This policy explains what information the app processes, why it is used, and the choices available to users.

Information we collect

  • Account details such as name, email address, password hash, and Google account link.
  • User content such as saved vocabulary, meanings, study notes, and text submitted to learning tools.
  • Authentication and security information needed to keep users signed in and verify email addresses.
  • When online-presence tracking is active, connection information for the live study-session features: IP address, device/browser user agent, and session start, end, and duration timestamps, linked to your account ID.

DLT does not store users’ plain-text passwords.

How information is used

  • To create, verify, secure, and maintain a DLT account.
  • To synchronize saved vocabulary and notes across signed-in devices.
  • To provide dictionary, translation, grammar, note-formatting, and story features.
  • To respond to account deletion and support requests and prevent misuse.
  • To show whether a friend is currently online and to keep the connection to our servers reliable (session/presence tracking).

Service providers and AI processing

DLT uses service providers to host the API and databases, authenticate Google accounts, deliver verification emails, and process AI-assisted learning requests. Text submitted to translation, grammar, dictionary, note-formatting, autofill, or story tools may be sent to the configured AI provider solely to generate the requested result.

Current service categories include cloud hosting, MongoDB-compatible account and vocabulary storage, Astra-compatible notes storage, Google authentication, Gmail email delivery, and Groq AI processing. These providers process information under their own privacy and security terms. DLT does not sell personal information.

Retention and deletion

Account data, vocabulary, and notes are retained while the account remains active. Unfinished email-registration records expire automatically. Session/presence records (IP address, user agent, timestamps) are automatically deleted after 90 days, and are also deleted immediately when an account is deleted. Users can permanently delete their account and associated data from Settings → Delete account.

Users who no longer have the app can use the account deletion page.

Security

DLT uses encrypted HTTPS connections for production network traffic, hashes passwords before storage, and uses access tokens to restrict account data to the signed-in user. No system can guarantee absolute security.

Children

DLT is intended for adult and older-teen language learners and is not directed to children under 13.

Your choices

Users may review their account email in Settings, remove saved content, log out, unlink access by deleting their account, or request complete account deletion.

Policy updates

This policy may be updated when DLT’s features or legal obligations change. The effective date at the top of this page will identify the latest version.

Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to the developer contact email displayed on DLT’s Google Play listing.
